BlitzVS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlitzVS collects, uses, and protects information when you use our services, website, and related platforms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information such as your username, email address, and profile details.
  • Usage data, including interactions with the app, feature usage, and diagnostic information.
  • Device and browser information, such as operating system, app version, and language settings.
  • Information you voluntarily provide, such as support requests or feedback.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
  • Respond to customer support requests and communications.
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience and product quality.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data only with trusted service providers who help operate our services, or when required by law, regulation, or legal process.

4. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies or similar technologies to understand usage, remember preferences, and improve site performance. You can control cookie settings through your browser.

5. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, updates, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law and operational requirements.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, however.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@blitzvs.example.
