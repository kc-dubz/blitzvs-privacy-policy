BlitzVS Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlitzVS handles information when you play the game. It applies to the current version of BlitzVS distributed through Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

The current version of BlitzVS is an offline game. It does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play.

BlitzVS does not currently include an advertising SDK or a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

The game saves settings locally on your device so that your preferences can be retained between sessions. Based on the current implementation, these settings are stored on the device rather than transmitted to a BlitzVS server.

This policy describes the current implementation and does not cover data collection by features or third-party services that have not yet been added.

2. How Information Is Used

Locally stored settings are used to remember your game preferences and support the normal operation of BlitzVS.

The current version does not use a separate analytics service to track gameplay or a separate crash-reporting service to collect crash reports.

3. Data Storage and Security

Game settings are stored locally on your device. Their security depends in part on your device's operating system, security settings, and physical access controls.

You can generally remove locally stored game data by clearing the game's storage through your device settings or uninstalling the game. Uninstalling may remove settings and other locally stored game data.

4. Third-Party Services and Data Sharing

The current version of BlitzVS does not include an advertising SDK and does not provide online multiplayer, online accounts, or online leaderboards.

Accordingly, these features do not currently send game data to advertising providers or BlitzVS online services because they are not included in this release.

This statement concerns the game's current features and integrations. It does not make claims about data that may be independently processed by the platform through which the game is installed or updated.

5. Children's Privacy and Intended Audience

BlitzVS is intended for teens and adults. The current version does not require players to submit personal information to play.

If the game's audience or features change, its privacy practices and applicable requirements will be reviewed and updated as necessary.

6. Future Features

Future versions of BlitzVS may introduce advertising, online multiplayer, player display names, accounts, cloud saves, or leaderboards.

These features are not described as current functionality. If they are introduced, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the information involved, how it is used and shared, and any applicable retention or deletion choices.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when BlitzVS features, integrations, or privacy practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an updated policy is published.

Please review this page periodically for updates.
